# What's New in Version 2.1

• Choose what is measured: the yellow frame on the preview shows the area each picture is measured from. Drag it to move it, or drag its handles to resize it. Each frame keeps its own area; Automatic Area goes back. A soft picture can be given more contrast by measuring only part of it.
• Better automatic tones: the edges of the scan no longer affect brightness, and small bright lights such as lamps, spotlights and sun glints no longer make the whole picture dark. Night scenes stay dark.
• Black-and-white: shadows keep their detail instead of going black, and the lower tones are a little more open, which leaves more room for editing. "By film exposure" now places black-and-white tones correctly for camera scans.
• Nearly colourless colour pictures, such as pale sky or grey sea, are recognised as colour more reliably.
• Output format and JPEG quality are remembered, like the output folder.
