# What's New in Version 2.1

• Choose what is measured: the yellow frame on the preview shows the area each picture is measured from. Drag it to move it, or drag its handles to resize it. Each frame keeps its own area; Automatic Area goes back.
• Better automatic tones: the edges of the scan no longer affect brightness, and small bright lights such as lamps, spotlights and sun glints no longer make the whole picture dark. Night scenes stay dark.
• Nearly colourless colour pictures, such as pale sky or grey sea, are recognised as colour more reliably.
